About the society

We are a student-run community that learns science by doing it: at the bench, at the keyboard and in the field.

Greenway Sci-Tech & Research Society began with a simple idea: science makes more sense when you do it with your own hands.

We run workshops, build projects and share what we learn, so every member leaves with something they made or discovered. Beginners are welcome, and so are people who already know their way around a lab.

Our work covers four areas: chemistry, biology, physics and technology, and research methods. Members move between them freely.

Mission

To make hands-on science open to every member and turn curiosity into work that others can read and build on.

Vision

A community where students design their own experiments, publish their findings and take on real problems.

Values

Curiosity, lab safety, honest data, and sharing what we learn.

How a project runs

Every project follows the same four steps, whatever the subject.

1

Ask a question

Pick something you genuinely want to find out and write it down clearly.

2

Plan the method

Decide what to measure, what to build and how to keep it safe.

3

Build and test

Run the experiment, record the data and repeat until the results hold up.

4

Share the results

Present at an event or write it up for our publications page.
